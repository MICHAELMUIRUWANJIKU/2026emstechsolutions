1 Who we are

EM'S Soft-Tech Solutions Kenya ("we", "us", "our") is a software company based in Kimbo, Ruiru, Kenya. We provide website design, mobile app development, graphic design, digital marketing, IT support, cyber services, SEO and work automation systems.

For the purposes of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, we are the data controller for personal information collected through this website and through our day-to-day business operations.

If you are viewing this page from outside Kenya, some of the rights described in section 9 may differ. We still apply the same standards of care to your data wherever you are.

2 Information we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to your enquiry and to run our business. This falls into three groups:

2.1 Information you give us voluntarily

  • Quote request form: your name, business name (optional), email address, phone number, preferred contact method, services of interest, budget range, project description, timeline, and any reference websites you mention.
  • WhatsApp / phone calls: your phone number and the content of the message or call.
  • Email enquiries: your email address, name and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address (if you choose to subscribe).

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and referring page — collected by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
  • Usage data: pages viewed and time on site (only if you enable analytics — see section 5).

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for, store or process any payment card details on this website.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal data (health, religious beliefs, political opinions, biometric data).
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 (see section 10).

3 How we use your information

We use your personal information only for the specific purposes below:

  • To respond to your enquiry — replying to your quote request by WhatsApp, email or phone call.
  • To prepare a quote or proposal — understanding your project so we can price it fairly.
  • To deliver services — if you become a client, to build, support and maintain what you've engaged us for.
  • To send occasional updates — only if you opted in to our newsletter (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To keep records — invoices, agreements and correspondence required for tax, accounting and legal compliance under Kenyan law.
  • To improve our website — using aggregated, anonymised usage statistics if analytics is enabled.

We will never sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4 Legal basis for processing

Under section 30 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Your consent — when you tick a box, submit the quote form, or subscribe to the newsletter. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract — to deliver services you have engaged us for.
  • Legitimate interest — responding to a business enquiry you initiated, and protecting our website from misuse.
  • Legal obligation — retaining financial and tax records as required by the Kenya Revenue Authority.

5 Cookies & analytics

When you first visit this website, a cookie consent banner appears. You can accept all cookies, reject non-essential ones, or manage them individually. Your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage and can be changed at any time via the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer.

This website uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary — always active. We store your theme (light/dark) and style preference in your browser's localStorage. These never leave your device and are not used for tracking.
  • Analytics — currently not active. If enabled in future, they will only load after you give consent, and will collect anonymised usage statistics.
  • Marketing — currently not active. If enabled in future, they will only load after you give consent.
  • Preference cookies — we store your chosen theme (light/dark) and style preference in your browser's localStorage. These are not sent to any server and are not used for tracking.
  • Analytics cookies (optional, not yet enabled) — if we enable Google Analytics in future, you will see a cookie consent notice before any analytics cookie is set. You will always be able to decline.

Action required: If you set up Google Analytics (or any other analytics tool), you must add a cookie consent banner before the site goes live. Ask us and we'll add one.

You can clear your stored preferences at any time via your browser settings. Doing so will simply reset your theme and style to the default.

6 When we share your information

We share your personal information only with trusted third parties who help us run our business, and only to the extent necessary:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — when you use our WhatsApp button or form to contact us, your message travels through WhatsApp's servers under their privacy policy.
  • Google LLC — our email, contact form and (if enabled) analytics run on Google infrastructure. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Website hosting provider — stores the site files and server logs for security and uptime.
  • Professional advisers — accountants or auditors, only where legally required.
  • Law enforcement — only if we are legally compelled by a valid Kenyan court order or statutory obligation.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, ever.

7 How long we keep your data

  • Unsuccessful enquiries — 12 months, then deleted.
  • Active client records — for the duration of our engagement plus 7 years, in line with Kenyan tax and record-keeping requirements.
  • Newsletter subscribers — until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days to process the unsubscribe.
  • Website server logs — typically 30 days, then rotated by our hosting provider.

When retention periods end, we securely delete or anonymise your data.

8 How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption on this website (SSL/TLS).
  • Strong passwords and two-factor authentication on our business accounts.
  • Access to your data limited to team members who need it to serve you.
  • Regular software updates and secure backups.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we do our best, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we ever become aware of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) without undue delay, as required by law.

9 Your rights under Kenyan law

Under sections 26–40 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be informed — of how your data is used (this policy).
  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct — ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete — ask us to erase your data where we have no lawful reason to keep it (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Object — to processing based on legitimate interest, or to direct marketing.
  • Restrict processing — ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint — with the ODPC (see section 14).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at michaelkey394@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days at no cost to you.
